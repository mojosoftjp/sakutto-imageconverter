Frequently asked questions

Can it convert iPhone photos (HEIC) to JPEG?

Yes. Add them to the Convert tab and choose JPEG. It opens them when Microsoft's "HEIF Image Extensions" and "HEVC Video Extensions" are installed in Windows. If they are not, the app shows you their Microsoft Store page.

Can it process dozens of images at once?

Yes. Select or drag and drop the files together, and they are processed one after another automatically.

Are my original images kept?

Yes. Convert, Resize and Compress always save new files. If a file with the same name exists, a number such as "_1" is added, so nothing is overwritten.

Can I remove location data from photos?

Yes. Turn on "Strip metadata" in the Compress tab to save without EXIF data (date taken, camera model, location and more).

Can I turn a transparent PNG into a JPEG?

Yes. In the Convert tab, transparent areas are filled with the background color you choose (white, black or any color).

Can I rename the output files in bulk?

Yes. Add text to the start or end of the name, or replace part of the original name. Your settings are kept for next time.

Does image processing work without an internet connection?

Yes. Everything from opening to saving happens on your PC.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Buy it once on the Microsoft Store and keep using it with no monthly fee.
